What is in the box

Every meme people keep buying through this cabinet, and how the claw does on each of them. One of them has never had a single fee carried out of it and the machine keeps it at the back where the glass is dirtiest.

How it works

four rules on the side of the cabinet

Nothing about this is hidden, which is the joke: everybody can read the rules and everybody keeps buying through it anyway.

1

The buy

Somebody buys a meme, and the claw drops into that exact trade looking for the fee. It does not care whose wallet it is, it does not ask first, and it gets exactly one attempt per buy.

one buy · one attempt · no queue
2

The grab

Grip strength is rolled on chain for every single attempt and most rolls are pathetic. Nobody tunes it, nobody can buy a better one, and the machine has no idea whose trade it is when it decides how badly to hold on.

rolled per attempt · nobody sets it
3

The drop

Whatever slips falls straight into the pile under the glass and stays there in plain sight. Nobody gets it back, nothing is refunded, and there is no second attempt on a trade that has already gone through.

everything it drops stays visible in the box
4

The payout

Anything that makes it all the way to the chute is split in the same block: half goes back to everyone holding $CLAW and the rest burns. Then the machine resets, a new meme drops into the box and the claw waits for the next buy.

half to holders · the rest burns
